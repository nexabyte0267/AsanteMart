Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay?

MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money, Visa/Mastercard, or Cash on Delivery within Ejisu & Greater Kumasi.

How fast is delivery?

Same-day rider delivery for Ejisu & Kumasi for orders placed before 3 PM. Accra, Takoradi, Tamale and other regions receive parcels within 24–48 hours.

Can I return an item?

Yes — unopened items can be returned within 7 days. Defective electronics are replaced or refunded after inspection.

Do you deliver outside Kumasi?

Yes, nationwide via partner couriers. Regional dispatch starts at GHS 55 plus per-item weight handling.

Is pickup really free?

Yes. Choose "Store Pickup" at checkout and collect from our Ejisu Hub or Adum point within 2 hours of confirmation.

Do you offer free rider delivery?

Yes — Same-City Rider delivery is free for cart totals of GHS 400 or more within Ejisu & Greater Kumasi.

Our Story

Built for Reliable Everyday Shopping in Ghana

Founded in Ejisu with dispatch hubs serving Greater Kumasi and nationwide parcel routes, AsanteMart bridges the gap between quality retail and fast doorstep delivery.

Why Shop With AsanteMart?

Online shopping shouldn't mean guessing whether an item is actually in stock or waiting days for local delivery. Every product listed on AsanteMart is physically stocked in our Ejisu warehouse and inspected before dispatch.

  • Real-Time Inventory: Live stock counts prevent frustrating post-order cancellations.
  • Fair Weight-Based Delivery: Light accessories travel cheap; heavy appliances are safely handled by dedicated riders and vans.
  • Transparent Pricing: All prices in Ghana Cedis (GHS) with zero hidden checkout fees.
